# Owner input required

Facts only the business owner can supply. **None of them are invented** — the
platform ships with clearly-marked placeholders and safe behaviour until each
is provided, per the project's owner-fact policy: engineering continues,
fabrication never.

How to read the table: _Safe temporary behaviour_ is what the system does
today without the fact. _Production blocker_ = the public NuESheba launch
cannot happen until it is supplied; **no** means launch can proceed and the
item can follow.

## Working agreement

- Engineering proceeds on everything that does not depend on a fact above.
- Placeholder values never migrate into marketing copy, JSON-LD claims, or
  legal text; where a fact is missing the feature renders nothing rather than
  something invented.
- `pnpm readiness` (the production-readiness check) is the executable form of
  this document: it fails while blocker-marked placeholders remain.
